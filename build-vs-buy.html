What building it yourself doesn't include

A coding agent can write your formulas. It does not run your math deterministically, govern it, or serve it to your people, your apps, and your agents from one source of truth. Check what you would actually need to build and operate. TrueMath includes all of it, plus the domain assistant, MCP server, and CLI.
