Introduction

Introduction to TrueMath

TrueMath turns your business logic into a private, executable domain: your formulas, your assumptions, your definitions. Every calculation runs against that domain, so the same inputs always produce the same auditable answer, whether the question comes from a person, an app, or an AI agent. These videos walk through the platform and each domain in the Professional Library.
